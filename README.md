# Showcase AI

**Autonomous AI Lead-Qualifier & RAG Sales Assistant**

Showcase AI is a business automation system designed to automate the qualification and handling of inbound B2B leads.

The system combines deterministic workflow logic with AI-based analysis, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), human approval, AI-generated email drafts, draft versioning and automated email delivery.

The project is built as a reusable technical showcase for AI automation, workflow orchestration and B2B process automation.

---

## 🎯 Business goal

The system is designed for companies receiving inbound business inquiries that require:

- qualification of incoming leads,
- identification of the relevant service,
- assessment of automation potential,
- human approval for important decisions,
- generation of personalized business responses,
- controlled editing of AI-generated drafts,
- automated email delivery.

The goal is not to let an LLM independently control the entire process.

AI is responsible for interpretation and generation, while deterministic workflow logic and PostgreSQL handle business rules, statuses and state transitions.

🤖 AI and RAG

The system uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation to provide the AI with relevant information from the company's knowledge base.

Knowledge base

The current knowledge base contains:

knowledge/

    └── services.md

Documents are indexed in Qdrant using embeddings generated by Ollama.

Current embedding model:

nomic-embed-text

Vector dimensions:

768

The RAG retriever currently uses:

topK = 6
🧠 Lead qualification

AI classification produces structured output:

MATCH
NO_MATCH
UNCERTAIN

For matching leads, the system identifies the relevant service:

AUTOMATION
INTEGRATIONS
LEAD_AUTOMATION
RAG
AI_AGENTS

The AI also returns:

confidence,
identified problem,
automation potential.

The final score is calculated deterministically by the workflow rather than by the LLM.

Current scoring model:

Confidence:
high   = 40
medium = 20
low    = 0

Automation:
high   = 40
medium = 20
low    = 0

Service identified:
+20

Maximum score:

100
🔀 Lead routing

The qualification result determines the next workflow state.

MATCH + score >= 80
        ↓
PENDING_APPROVAL

MATCH + score < 80
        ↓
REVIEW

UNCERTAIN
        ↓
REVIEW

NO_MATCH
        ↓
UNQUALIFIED

This separation keeps business decisions deterministic and auditable.

👤 Human-in-the-Loop

Important lead decisions are handled through Telegram.

The system supports:

lead approval,
lead rejection,
manual review,
draft approval,
draft editing,
draft rejection.

Telegram acts as the human approval interface while n8n remains responsible for workflow orchestration.

✉️ AI-generated email drafts

Approved leads are passed to:

AI - Generowanie draftu odpowiedzi

The AI generates:

email subject,
complete email body.

The generated draft is stored in PostgreSQL.

The system explicitly prevents the AI from inventing:

prices,
timelines,
technical specifications,
company information,
unsupported business claims.
📝 Draft versioning

Email drafts are versioned in PostgreSQL.

Example:

version 1 → AI
version 2 → AI_EDIT
version 3 → AI_EDIT

Every edit creates a new database record instead of overwriting the previous version.

This provides a basic audit trail of AI-generated and human-requested modifications.

🗄️ PostgreSQL

PostgreSQL stores the business state of the system.

Main tables:

leads
lead_drafts
draft_edit_sessions

The leads table stores qualification and workflow state.

The lead_drafts table stores versioned email drafts.

The draft_edit_sessions table controls active Telegram editing sessions.

📧 Email delivery

Email delivery uses SMTP.

Current development setup uses Gmail SMTP.

The sender address is configured in n8n and is not stored in the repository.

The workflow only changes the lead status to:

SENT

after the email delivery step succeeds.

SMTP errors are handled separately and do not trigger the SENT state.

🛡️ Error handling

The workflow contains dedicated error paths for important AI and infrastructure operations.

Examples include:

AI draft generation errors,
AI draft editing errors,
SMTP delivery errors.

Errors are reported through Telegram instead of silently allowing the workflow to continue as if the operation succeeded.
